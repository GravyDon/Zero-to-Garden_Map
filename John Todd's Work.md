Found through the reading of parent::[[The Vertical Farm Book Notes Fall 2025]].

John Todd was a Canadian eco-scientist prominent in the 80's, created a farm on which energy cycling was an active ingredient in their designs 

- Fertilizers go into carrots and worm mass
- Worm mass goes into fish 
- Fish waste goes into fertilizers

- Carrots go into rabbits
- Rabbit waste goes into soils which goes into...
- Carrots and worms :)

A pretty simple system, but the real sticking point is the technology that was built on site to facilitate these goals friend::[documentary on this farm](https://www.nfb.ca/film/new_alchemists/)
- Windmill using barrels that are split in half making a vertical shaft which funnels the air. Attached to a pedal mechanism which processes two sets of wires to actuate a wooden arm which raises or depresses a piston
- Refrigerator linings being used as clarification tanks where nitrate processing bacteria can process waste from fish to either recycle the water back into the tank or use for fertilizer. 
- Cultivation of midges for feeding fishes (mosquito like but not; more benign)
- Creation of a geodesic dome to heat fish pond (or a sort of greenhouse); also lower level domes
- Creation of windmill sails :0 and windmills through the usage of a bicycle wheel and some flaps
