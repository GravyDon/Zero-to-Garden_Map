A mud house capable of storing materials and people, but does not have much in the ways of comfort. Sustains the internal organic matter inside of the wall providing food source per unit area of the walls. Drip irrigation sustains the supply of nutrients, miniscule organic matter. Drip irrigation is supplied by vertical layer machine OR it could work off of the roof biome drainage. Electrical systems will sustain any writing/reading, cooking, and any non-internet activities done in the home as well as the drip irrigation system. Other food can be derived from the roof biome with some modest roof access OR root access in a roof compartment. 

Depending on the mycelial strain, mushrooms could grow on the inside as well (i.e. puff ball mushrooms would make a soft and manageable fruit body that would not cause continual respiratory irritation if managed properly rather than constantly having to manage spore production by premature annulus identification and subsequent fruiting body removal)
