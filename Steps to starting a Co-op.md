"A producer cooperative use the cooperative to market or process their agricultural goods, arts and crafts, or other products."

##### Co-op principles
==Going to want these in the NCGC presentation==
1. Voluntary and open membership
2. Democratic member control
3. Member economic participation
4. Autonomy and Independence
5. Education, training, and information
6. Cooperation among cooperatives
7. Concern for community 

##### Phases of Co-op development
1. ==Organizing== (3 - 12 month period)
2. ==Start up (usually a 6 month phase)== **focus on these two for now...**
3. Growth
4. Consolidation (different from capitalism)
	"The phase in which the worker-owners assess their progress in reaching the targeted growth and develop the firm's goals and strategies for the next 3-5 years"
		This can be a stage at which we choose to grow the business in an ethical way using community sourced labor and community oriented ends (cycle continues with little to no extraction)

##### Responsibilities of worker members
1. take the full risks and benefits of working owning and operating their cooperative business
2. **equitably** contribute to and benefit from the capital of their cooperative
3. decide how the net income, or net losses, are allocated
4. govern and control the enterprise on the basis of one member-one vote, by consensus decision making, or other democratic structure
5. work together (as opposed to being independent contractors) in a commonly owned business
