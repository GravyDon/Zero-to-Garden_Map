"grow" lights, as you will search for, are a scam. They are priced based on their function and not their specficiations. Furthermore, if you are crafty, it is possible to get an ample supply of lighting LED's, or even flourescent lights, as long as you know how to supply power to them (electronics). Avoiding the grow light branding will save you a significant amount of money. 

Now, what about the luminosity of the grow lights? Will any old LED light work? In short, no there is a commercial standard which was likely observed through trial/error. **500-100 lumens per square foot (lux) is a suitable number for most purposes of growing indoors.** 

What I do is a find lights that have a high lumen per watt count, thus minimizing energy costs. Calculating the flux from luminosity, especially at the distances that are required between grow lights and plants, would require integration, and I might come back to this and do the calculations, but no. 

Instead, it's best to get a dimmable grow light, or attach a potentiometer rated to the voltage and current of the grow light, and control the intensity until the plants are happy. **Too much light can harm the plants or lead to duds.**
