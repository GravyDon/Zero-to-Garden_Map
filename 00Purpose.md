## Motivations
**Definition:** *unigrated agroindustry is a set of socially observant production practices aimed at unifying people and nature towards a healthier, more sustainable relationship. The production itself is concerned with the production and transmutation of natural capital into food, medicine, building materials, chemical reagents, fibers, dyes, and fuels. This form of production does not exclude on the basis of privilege through seeking a varied approach towards agricultural production, encouraging communal, low-tech, and/or decentralized solutions. Unifying practices in this effort involve the accessibility of knowledge, material, community outlets and inlets, and the methods of a successful implementation supported on a maximally challenging (or minimal) budget in the current and unfolding economy.* 

Mutual aid, socialism, anarchist, and ecological principles guide this mode of production: a response to the unique circumstances which present under late stage capitalism. The form of production presented seeks to be a gate into directly re-seizing the control of material which mediates our daily lives, the seizure of which will alleviate the burden of an exploitative system through the collective efforts of a community to feed, cloth, medicate, and power itself and its people. Through these times of hyper-capitalist exploitation, government corruption, labor devaluation, and lack of unity, the capacity to exist on our own, locally-sourced and cycled material will be a key shift in how we relate to the greater economy. This key step will set the stage for cultural, social, epidemiological, psychosomatic, and spiritual shifts that can show us what is possible beyond the current level of care that is being provided to the lower classes.  

The time is now to come together to produce the needs that are being unmet by the people who control supply chains without regard to qualitative outcomes. America's means of production are more nebulous than ever; very few people ever get to interact with said means and those that do are either overseas or already legally invested into total compliance with the true masters of material production. Although there is still room for rebellion, as is the case with all slave and master dynamics, the ideal of reparations from the rich and redistribution of wealth should not be the only eventuality that we are counting on for the sake of finally creating equity. We can choose to re-create our own means of production that do not antagonize each other, the planet, our ancestors, or the space around us. The ability to do so has implications that are far reaching and will re-contextualize the struggle against the elite[^4]. 

Starting from our hierarchy of needs, we must first provide for our own health and good living. Food, medicine, building materials, and access to chemicals which can be used for heating, cooling, cleaning, preserving, and acid-base reactions will put us in a position to win back key capital vested within a system which holds us all hostage. From there, it is up to us to continue to fight until elite power structures cannot sustain their control on their technological means of production and oppression. At the current moment, we cannot even imagine taking a day off from work to protest peacefully; protesting with force for a demand is out of the question, yet such tactics were alive and well not even a decade ago. While a resurgence might be possible, the network of support that is required to sustain resistance is still at the mercy of direct influence by elites through market manipulation. Our tenacity is being choked by the boot of the free market and its demands of our freedom; there will be no time left when times become even tougher as a result of the Iran war drying up the national oil reserves and effectively stagnating the incoming oil supply for another few months. Fertilizer, fuel, and petroleum products are going to plummet in supply and soar in demand, and thus we will be facing one of the hardest economies of the 21st century in conjunction with the AI bubble popping and the job market tanking. Our very livelihoods have been in the hands of those who would sooner see us on the streets than in a house; people who would rather see us full of diseases and toxic chemicals than eating and drinking from the bounty of the Earth's bosom; there are people who are sleeping soundly knowing that they are causing multiple genocides, and they wake up to complain about the mundane as if they need to be more entitled to comfort. Their comfort is unreasonable and ours is deserved, yet they will spin this truth exactly around: our comforts are a luxury while theirs is a given. Bullshit! We deserve to have good things, even if that means we must obtain them through a different means than they do. When they go low, we go high! We should create an economic and manufacturing base that does not rely on exploitation and rather on sustainable principles to promote a healthier reality where everyone can live in harmony, with all being given an equitable situation to progress through life uninhibited. 

Yet there is a transition period that cannot be glossed over with passion and youthful vigor. Too much time into this new system of production, *without a total solution towards sustainable living*, will result in tension between worlds placed upon the shoulders of the most active co-creators. The old world, is stuck with systems of connection that rely upon consumerism, commodification of self, the seizing and hoarding of power[^1] (which causes much of the history of oppressive practices in racism, sexism, queerphobia, xenophobia, materialism, etc.), and ever-increasingly violent cycles of oppression, resistance, rupture, re-establishment, and relaxation. Stepping out of the old-world leaves behind its cycles that will eventually lead to the fall of humanity in overwhelming hatred beyond transmutation[^2], ecological disaster, sociopolitical cataclysms, and the dissolution of trust in each other. At first, the burden of transitioning into this new world will be most sorely felt upon the shoulders of pioneering individuals. They will burden the tension of this first period of transition in the experimental phase. Straddling the role of playing our given role in the market and co-creating a new system of economic relations will take its mental, social, and physical toll. However, one cannot expect to win a war spanning millennia (the war between classes) without sacrifice. Yet, if the sacrifices are made, and the opportunities for establishing a new, communally sourced economy are seized, inevitably, the tide will shift in favor of collective responsibility for burdening the weight of co-creation. People will have more time to invest into a system which meets their basic needs. More genuine time invested into the system means a more competent structure is formed to support more people. A more competent structure then leads to more people being able to invest their full-attention towards the creation of economic, cultural, and social independence from the old world. Eventually an emergent structure that will meet even more needs and build more resistance capacity will form! 

Furthermore, we live in times of stagnation, but that stagnation is largely in part to how we have been forced to live with each other: physically apart and without soulful connection. 

 Wholesome interpersonal and worldly connections are possible when formed through passions for making food, medicines, shared spaces, restoring the environment, inventing for collective good, and memories of the season's successes and failures  (among a million other things made possible in a more compassionate world). Meaning will be restored in our lives when the meaning of life is to be in the loving presence of those freeing themselves from oppression. The model for freeing ourselves proposed here is distinct in that it is wholly accessible to everyone on an ideologically neutral level. Everyone can agree that being able to produce your own materials independent of the current impending disasters is a great idea. 
 
 The knowledge that is contained within this repository will hopefully come to your aid in realizing a practice of unigrated agroindustry. Meet your earthly needs and connect with a higher purpose of continuing the compassionate nature of Earth to all of its children. Hopefully your passion for implementing and building this vault, when balanced with other worldly responsibilities, will lead to a communal spaces that cannot be replicated or shaken by the stochastic terror of bourgeoisie economists, politicians, and their cronies. Even if the elite billionaires did destroy all worldly possessions gathered to realize the plans set forth, the knowledge and potential would remain to threaten their lies of a world only possible through the filter of their tyranny.

**Zero-waste** is the core principle throughout this vault. Material is explicitly produced in a manner that makes it reusable, recyclable, and/or renewable. The only exception might be certain higher-tech infrastructure at the edges of a successful implementation of the model of agroindustry laid forth. The reason zero-waste is so key to the goals of this model of production is that extraction as the source of material is the privilege of the wealthy. Extraction to sustain societies is only possible when there is someone else suffering a loss to facilitate our gain; then we throw out what was used despite value still being present. Creating a mode of production which minimizes and works towards all material being reusable means that more people can be supported on less land for a longer period of time. It's surprising to learn that there exist many technologies which facilitate the process of recycling nutrients and material, yet they are glossed over for the ever-more convenient solution of simply throwing out what is considered unfit for consumption.

Technological solutions which necessitate the spraying of chemicals that can cause cancer, diseases, and generally are not needed due to an alternative being found in organic means of plant remediation can be critically cut out from this practice[^3]. Only in the case of hyper-specific, controlled situations should such demonstrably terrible dousing of the planet in toxic molecules be seriously considered, and in all other respects barred from polluting the planet. This also applies for toxic usages of fertilizers, monocultures, and the phasing out of certain trophic layers and/or kingdoms of life. Tilling soil can be mitigated or avoided depending on the soil and available methods for remediating soil for an agricultural model. Usage of the combustion engine and heavy machinery might still be required, but, ideally, the democratizing practices championed by unigrated forestry should reduce the environmental burden via reserving this practice rather than being the standard or creating fuels and/or engines which burn cleanly. 

**Compassion for the environment** is a core principle in rejecting the current practices to seek out new ways to produce our natural resources within communities. In respecting diversity, a diversity of tactics is necessarily valued to seek out flexible, interesting, and adaptable solutions, thus allowing practices to grow in any space, time, or context.

**Definition:** unigrated metaforestry is the modeling of human/natural or otherwise meaning-convergent spaces with ideas of forestry. The model emulates that of a forest, in its layering of energy cycles in vertical and horizontal space with overlapping forms corresponding with overlapping functions. It is the prioritization of diversity in life and the cultivation of an overall sustainable system of progress and succession. The benefits of a forest metaphor in aligning organization of human activity is mostly contextual: stemming from the principles which guide and direct the flow of energy in a forest and modeling human behaviors to distribute (energy/resources/information/practice) through the community. There are also some phenomena I see relevant to the idea of organization effectively, such as the idea of succession in a forest before certain seeds can grow in the environment and contribute a diverse patterning to community action. These metaphorical mechanics are also strained, but a useful lens to view sustainable systems from.  Perhaps one day, we can rebuild our own forests to care for our bodies passively as we make our way through brighter days.

[^1]: Power is used in the most general sense here: the ability to move mass or energy. Whether that energy is tied up in human motion or motion of naturally occurring entities (i.e. rivers, lakes, atmosphere, oil, etc.) is not important, but the inclusivity of power here for direct control or forcing of all phenomena of motion is important to distinguish what power means and how to exert it responsibly. Intuitively, one can move a rock responsibly in many ways. Throw it, break it, drop it, pick it up; there are no right or wrong answers in a vacuum with a rock and your biological capacity to move it. Ethics of power come into play when dealing with power over human motion immediately however. Ethical laws of power do not exist in a capitalist society whose objective is frequently tied to ideals of "profit over people", "the faster the better", "might is right", etc. Starting from a mode of production that prioritizes ethical relationships with the source of production is essential at the root of it all. Simply looking back to plantations and our reliance on slave labor to lift America out of a colonial wonder to an independent powerhouse is one example of non-ethical, dehumanizing relationships, both with people and nature, that continues to echo its relational dynamics through today's economic and social relations. The same is true of the genocide of Native Americans, whose culture held nature in an ethical relationship of production, but perhaps lacked the tools to expand their tribes past their limited carrying capacity. While we may have had the tools to expand production, we ended up destroying 99% of the ecological density on this continent in less than 200 years; this ecological wonder of abya yala had been blooming over the course of 1000's of years only to be cut down in the fraction of time by power wielded in the hands of the morally justified colonizer. 

[^2]: Hatred beyond transmutation is a tricky observable to define, but is obvious once it is contextualized with the act of transmutation or loving action. Thus, at what point within a culture does an act of hatred only lead to more hatred regardless of intention? Hatred for the sake of "fighting a common enemy" such as WWII still lead to the events of the Cold War in the Treaty of Versailles, the creation of the atom bomb, the deaths of millions of soldiers, and scars that have yet to heal on the Earth and within the psyches of its people. The same is true of anti-fascism, yet we accept that praxis against fascism must be disciplined enough to fight communities that must be protected. Focusing on the loving protection rather than the hateful destruction side of antifascist action will allow for the transmutation of hateful behaviors and prevent them from becoming rationale towards action rather than reflection. Hate is poison, but poison can either make us more resilient to future poisons or detriment our health permanently all the way up to death. Reflection on hatred is healthy and can lead to action seeking to protect; hateful action is venomous. The difference might seem pedantic, but one is a conscious decision to avoid hate from being associated with one's praxis and the other is a reaction. Both do not ignore hatred, but internalize; only one kind of action transmutes the hate into positive feeling and action. Once a society has descended past the capacity of addressing an event without overwhelming hatred, only ruin will follow. If the hatred continues to mount with each reactive event, the most likely outcome is the dissolving of trust and mayhem as people fracture along their reactions to chaotic ruptures within their nation and life within it.
