### Description 
Height: 0.5 - 0.6m
Bright orange, radial flowers on terminal stems (one bloom per stem)
Leaves are alternating and ovate of a few inches (2-3ish)

### How to Grow

PFAF says that this plant is somewhat resilient and can grow in many different types of soil; light, medium, or heavy
**Be careful of slugs, powdery mildew, and snails that will predate young plants.**
does not require fertilizer!! Mild amounts will be sufficient if inclined
##### Soil preparation
- Water need: moist soil
- Sun needs: Semi-sun (3-4 hours) to full sun
- Ph Level: 4.5-8.3

##### Germination
- Ideal germination temp: 50-59

##### Sowing
- Sowing time: direct sow just before last frost OR start indoors 
- Sowing depth: 1/4"  
- Sowing spacing: 12-18" spacing
##### Harvesting
- Ideal Harvest time: 60-70 days after sprouting
- Harvest Period: 10 days
##### Propagation and control
Propagation: Seeds 
Control: N/A

### Seasonal Periods

- Blooming time: June to November
Hardy level: level 4 to zone 6

## Uses

- Similar nutritional value to dandelion (good for salads) with a peppery taste to the petals and a pungent aroma present in the rest of the plant
	High in vitamins A and C
- Yellow dye

##### Medicinal properties from PFAF
"It is, above all, a remedy for skin problems and is applied externally to bites and stings, sprains, wounds, sore eyes, varicose veins etc" - Herpatic, astringent, antiseptic
"The whole plant, but especially the flowers and the leaves, is antiphlogistic, antiseptic, antispasmodic, aperient, astringent, cholagogue, diaphoretic, emmenagogue, skin, stimulant and vulnerary" 
## Ecological functions
- Attracts hoverflies which are natural predators of species of native aphids
- Attracts pollinators such as bees and butterflies
