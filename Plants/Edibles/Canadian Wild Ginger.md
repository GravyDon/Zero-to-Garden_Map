### Description 
Height: 0.1 - 0.5m (growing at a slow rate)

The root of the plant is round and fleshy (like ginger), with dividing stem supporting a heart-shaped, deep green above and light below, soft, wooly, and deeply veined leaf, there being two to a plant. The flower is one to a plant, small and of a brownish purple color, growing only a few inches high and sometimes becoming covered by the dead leaves that carpet the woods. The petals are fleshy in character.

### How to Grow
##### Soil preparation
- Water need: 1-inch water per week 
- Sun needs: Can grow in full shade or semi-shade
- Ph Level: 6-7.5

##### Germination
- Ideal germination temp: 64F / 18C

##### Sowing
- Sowing time: Late winter or early spring **with cold stratification**
- Sowing depth: 1/4"
- Sowing spacing: 12"
##### Harvesting
Ideal Harvest time: Autumn
- Harvest Period: All year round
##### Propagation and control
Propagation: Division
Control: Due to its ability to send out runners, both on the surface and underground, it is important to either place a competing species at the same root level OR only plant in areas that it can be properly maintained/prevented from taking over an entire yard. **POTENTIALLY AGGRESSIVE INVASIVE!!**

##### Medicinal properties from PFAF

##### Medicinal properties from Indian Herbology
Stimulant, carminative, tonic, diaphoretic, diuretic 
"As a carminative, it is useful in all painful spasms of the bowels and stomach" (antispasmodic of digestive tract)
Effective for nervous fatigue for expecting mothers during the second and third trimesters, BUT not close to birth due to anti-clotting properties [- from this source](https://www.kghypnobirthing.com/hypnobirthing-information/planning-your-birth/herbs-during-pregnancy-labour-and-birth) and also cited in Indian Herbology

From Russian research, the plant has additional uses as a cure for jaundice, dropsy, heart trouble (?), lung tuberculosis, migraine, headache, constipation, alocoholism, weakness and lack of stamina in children, and excites nerves, promotes milk in mothers 
## Ecological functions
- Can shade out the ground and compete for surface roots. 
- Provides a water barrier that can cool the ground and keep it humid in the hot summer months and heat during the cooler fall months (specific heat of water property).
