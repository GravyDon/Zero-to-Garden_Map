### How to Grow
##### Soil preparation
- Water need: well-drained soil (more sandy texture -> Loam, Sandy-loam loamy-sand) 
- Sun needs: #sun/full_sun; it cannot grow in the shade
- Ph Level: prefers lightly acidic soil (5.5-6.0), but can grow in the range 5.5 - 8

##### Germination
- Ideal germination temp: 70 - 75F

##### Sowing
- Sowing time: Early Spring (march)
- Sowing depth: 1/4in
- Sowing spacing: 2 - 4 inches

NOTE from [[Dill#Sources|source 1]]:
	- it is advised to replant every 2-4 weeks to keep it ready for fresh use or essentially replanting so as to produce the same species into the future. 
	- **Not advisable to start indoors due to the long taproot.**
##### Harvesting
Ideal Harvest time: For leaves, just before flowering. For seed, in the fall.
- Harvest Period: harvest the leaves at any time the plant has established itself. Seed can be harvested in midsummer to late fall
40 - 55 days
