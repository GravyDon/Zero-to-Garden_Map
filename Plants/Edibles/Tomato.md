There are thousands of types of tomatoes: cherry tomatoes, beefsteak tomatoes, bush tomatoes, vining tomatoes. You need to choose a subset of tomatoes of different layer types for layering into different gardens.

Two main types: 
~~**Determinate Tomatoes** or bush tomatoes grow 2-3 feet tall with variets that provide numerous ripe tomatoes at a time. Do not produce much foliage and tend to fruit for a brief period of time, but fruit earlier than vining varieties of tomatoes.~~

==**indeterminate Tomatoes** or vining varieties produce the largest mid-to-late season slicing tomatoes all summer and until the first frost.
