### Description 
Height: 0.9m or about 3ft

Key features include elliptical leaves arranged in a "ladder-like" sequence without petioles (connected directly to main stem). Formation of bell flowers in singles or triplets along the underside of the stem containing five stamen. Formation of blue berries upon fertilization

### How to Grow
##### Soil preparation
- Water need: Moderate
- Sun needs: Prefers partial sun, but can grow in the deep shade of a dense woodland canopy
- Ph Level: 6.0 - 7.0

##### Germination
- Ideal germination temp: NA

##### Sowing
- Sowing time: Fall
- Sowing depth: 1/2in
- Sowing spacing: 24 in
##### Harvesting
Harvest the root for medicinal purposes. Harvest the shoot for food.
Ideal Harvest time: Harvest root in autumn (September - October); Harvest shoot in early spring (March - Apr)
##### Propagation and control
Propagation: Rhizomes and berries (seed). 
	Intetionally spread via division: dig up a clump leaving some rhizomes uncut at the boundaries (keeping buds intact). Replant at same depth as soon as possible. 
	Seeds take 2-3 years to reach flowering size.
Control: cut back rhizome tips; berries will spread seeds, so either pluck flowers before they are fertilized or create 

rhizomes do not spread aggressively (6-12 inches per year of rhizome spread)

### Seasonal Periods

Blooming time: May-June
Fruiting time: Mid-late summer 
Hardy level: 4 in zones 3-8

## Uses
The medicinal properties of plants within the polygonatum genus, as according to the internet, is somewhat varied. The list of properties is fairly long. Although it was originally used by the Native Americans within their folk medicine, it was soon spread to Europe and then used within medicine of the middle ages. Chinese medicine documents usage of plants within the same genus for treating mental exhaustion, a kidney and liver tonic, and treating PTSD.

**ONLY THE YOUNG SHOOTS OR ROOT (in small amounts) can be eaten. Survival food source.**

##### Medicinal properties from PFAF
Can be applied externally in a poultice to treat bruises and small wounds. Can be used to remove freckles (???). Tonic for the heart and cardiovascular system, diuretic, hypoglycaemic, opthalmalic, antitussive, and demulcent when taken internally. Only medically active part of plant is the rhizome which is readily available and in abundant supply.
