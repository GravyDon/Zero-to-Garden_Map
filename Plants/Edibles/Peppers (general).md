### Description 
Height: 1m 

Evergreen shrub with small white blossoms whose fruits vary wildly depending on the cultivar 
- [[Cayenne]] - Longum fruits; spicy and up to 30cm long
- [[Bell peppers]] - Grossum or box shaped fruit with no spicy taste

### How to Grow
##### Soil preparation
- Water need: moist and well draining
- Sun needs: full sun
- Ph Level: 5.5 - 6.8

##### Germination
- Ideal germination temp: 

##### Sowing
- Sowing time: since this is a tropical plant, it is best grown via transplant in late april for zone 7b -> sow indoors and transfer into its respective position in the garden after the 15th of April (sow indoors 6-8 weeks before April 15th - >**March 4th*)
- Sowing depth: 
- Sowing spacing: 12in - 3ft
##### Harvesting
Ideal Harvest time:
- Harvest Period:

Fruits form 2-6 days after the flower drops. Harvest regularly not letting too many fruits stay on the branches; once fruits start to show some color change and are at a suitable length (80% of total possible) it is reasonable to harvest 
##### Propagation and control
Propagation: the plant is self-fertile 
Control: NA, **but since it is disease prone, watch out for pathogens that might use it as a host to get to other plants in the garden**

### Seasonal Periods

Blooming time: July - September
Growing Time: Mar - Apr (indoors) -> Apr - October (outside) = 210 days till harvest
Hardy level: 2

## Uses
Besides usage of sweet varieties as food, spicier varieties have various medicinal properties [[Cayenne]]

## Ecological roles
- the small white flowers have a lot of nectar
- Invertebrate shelter for overwintering and ground cover due to the lolling leaves
- The smell of the pepper plants can confuse some pests -> follow companion plantings

# Connections
[[Okra]] & [[Basil]] Both of these are good companion plantings (refer to book recommended by PFAF)
