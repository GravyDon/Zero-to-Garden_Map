### How to Grow
##### Soil preparation
- Water need: regular watering without waterlogging
- Sun needs: Full Sun
- Ph Level: 6.0–7.5

##### Germination
- Ideal germination temp: **64-72F**

**DO NOT BURY SEEDS! LIGHT IS REQUIRED FOR GERMINATION!!** Cold stratification will increase germination.
##### Sowing
- Sowing time: March 
- Sowing depth: 1/8"
- Sowing spacing: 12" (might be more due to aggressive root system)

If starting outside, it is preferable to spread the seeds on the surface during fall.
If starting inside, transfer once the plant has reached 5-8cm and, ofc, after the danger of frost is gone.

**VIRULENT LIKE MOST MEMBERS OF THE MINT FAMILY**
##### Harvesting
Ideal Harvest time: right before flowering is the ideal time to harvest leaves for max flavor.
- Harvest Period: 180 days (leaves); **Late Spring to Early Autumn**
##### Propagation and control
Propagation: self-fertile

If it becomes invasive, simply snip off flower heads to prevent seeding as there is no rhizome

### Seasonal Periods

Blooming time: **Early Summer to Early Fall**
Growing Time
Hardy level: zone 3 - 8; fully hardy up to zone 4

## Uses

All parts medicinally used by Native Americans for sores (poultices), masticated for tooth aches and sore throats, and can be used to treat coughs, diarrhoea, chest pains, fevers in children, stomach aches, and more
For afflictions of the mind such as, depression, insomnia, melancholy, hysteria, and nervousness

[[Lemon Balm#Sources|Source 1]] XVIII THE MOON - Pisces - a calming and guiding presence in the emotional flow that is mediated beyond our thoughts. Brings emotional stability in change.

Leaves are the most flavorful and medicinal part of the plant. Long lasting fragrance.
##### Recipes
1. Steep leaves in hot milk for five minutes then adding maple syrup; a nice nighttime drink :)
2. [[Lemon Balm#Sources|Source 1]] : Moon tea; cold infusion in water for 2 - 6 hours in the moonlight. Place in connection with earth or earthern works.
##### Medicinal properties from PFAF
The leaves and young flowering shoots are antibacterial, antispasmodic, antiviral, carminative, diaphoretic, digestive, emmenagogue, febrifuge, sedative, and tonic
Externally, it is used to treat herpes, sores, gout, insect bites and as an insect repellent
The essential oil contains citral and citronella, which act to calm the central nervous system and are strongly antispasmodic

## Ecological functions
- Drought tolerant, Insect Attractor, Pest Repellant, Nectary, provides cover for invertebrates/bugs

# Connections
- Plant near fruiting plants for more pollination
- Companion plant with brassicas family and any plant that can benefit from pest repellant properties
