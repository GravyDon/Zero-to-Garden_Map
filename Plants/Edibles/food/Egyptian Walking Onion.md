### Description 
Height: 1.2m 
Allium like stems that kink and grow out to form dense heads of edible, <=4cm diameter bulbs. The plant gets its name by the fact that it reseeds by "walking", placing its bulbs to the soil as they get heavy and rooting at the spot the onion droops.

### How to Grow
[[Egyptian Walking Onion#Sources|From source 2]]
 Signs of nitrogen deficiency: yellowing stalks
 Signs of phosphorous deficiency: light-green, slow-growing stalks (all stages) and browned tips and dead heads early into growing season (flowering stage and fruiting stage)
##### Soil preparation
- Water need: Moist soil, **but well drained**. An inch a week suffices ([[Egyptian Walking Onion#Sources|from source 2]])
- Sun needs: full sun
- Ph Level: slightly acid to slightly alkaline (extremes 4.5 - 8.3 [[Egyptian Walking Onion#Sources|According to PFAF]])

##### Germination
- Ideal germination temp: NA

##### Sowing
- Sowing time: Anytime throughout the year after the ground has thawed. Ideal in fall. 
- Sowing depth: 1/2 - 2 inch depending on the size of the bulb
- Sowing spacing: 9 - 14 inches apart
##### Harvesting
Ideal Harvest time: Summer - early fall
- Harvest Period: Throughout the year as the heads develop to the desired size. Pluck before the stalk droops to the ground. Can harvest shoots in the early winter or very-early spring OR anytime for the greens (leaves)
- Parts for harvest: aerial bulbs, roots, and leaves
##### Propagation and control
Propagation: bulbs
Control: Since the plants tend to clump, and their method of propagation is highly visible, there control simply involves harvesting. No invasive threat.

## Uses
food, poultice, and chemicals can be harvested via a solvent that can be used as a protective spray
good flavoring for pickles!

##### Recipes
- Boil the bulbs to extract an aromatic chemical which irritates pests and provides soluble sulfur to the leaves. after cooling, apply with a spray of the plant being protected. 

##### Medicinal properties from PFAF
- A poultice of baked onions acts as a #plantrole/medicine/Vulnerary 
- A diet including allium is good for the mouth, gut, and circulatory system.

## Ecological functions
- Pest repellant (Allium genus)
- Attracts insects

# Connections
- Does not grow well with legumes or [[alfalfa]]
- Grows well with roses, carrots, beets, and chamomile.
