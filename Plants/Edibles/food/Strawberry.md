### Description 
Height: 
An herbaceous ground cover with distinct, toothed, trifoliate, orbicular leaves. The stems emanate from a common point called the crown in which the roots emanate from. Stolons, or runners, emanate from this initial growth point to propagate the plant. Roots are shallow (**making it susceptible to drought within the first 6-12 inches of soil depending on the species rooting pattern**)

### How to Grow
Varieties are sensitive to their local conditions. Only select cultivars that are known to be well-established in the climate that you occupy with the proper soil conditions present.
##### Soil preparation
- Water need: High
- Sun needs: can grow in full sun or partial shade
- Ph Level: acidic to mildly alkaline (5.5 - 8.0)

##### Germination
- Ideal germination temp: 60-70F

##### Sowing
- Sowing time: *for transplanting*, do in april or early may. 
- Sowing depth: *for transplanting* bury up to the crown. 
- Sowing spacing: 18-24 inches apart

Due to heat sensitivity, it is recommended to mulch after to ensure that there is little heat stress and water is preserved. 

NOTE: strawberries are allelopathic and will build up harmful amounts of toxins to future varieties. There exist plants like [[Sorrel]] which can help in removing these toxins and create a longer lasting perrenial.
##### Harvesting
Ideal Harvest time: 28-30 days after full bloom. pick in the early morning. 
- Harvest Period: *for day-neutral varieties*, harvest throughout the season. *June bearing strawberries* produce in June and will need a season to be established before they can do so. 
##### Propagation and control
Propagation: Stolons
Control: Prune old (>4 years old) mother strawberries after harvest for june-bearing strawberries. For day-neutral strawberries, you will have to replace each plant 3-years out from first harvest. 

### Seasonal Periods

Blooming time: Depends on the variety. Certain varieties have been cultivated to bloom earlier in the season while others bloom later. 
Growing Time: Depends on cultivar, but the plants need a season to be established before producing if planting in the late summer. Plant in early spring for a harvest within the year. 
Hardy level: 

For the winter, it is important to either insulate using a hoophouse and/or provide a mulch layer to prevent soil freezing in the top layer and soil heaving.

## Ecological functions
Allelopathic, so be careful when planting with other plants AND try to plant in soils with large CEC to dilute allelopathic chemicals in the soil colloids.
