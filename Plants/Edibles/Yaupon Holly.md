### Description 
Height: up to 6m (PFAF) or 12-45ft but usually no more than 25 (Lady Bird)
![](https://www.wildflower.org/image_archive/640x480/CBR/CBR_IMG16330.JPG)
Found in South-Eastern United States; commonly used for landscaping
Dense clusters of red berries along branch; duos of berries coming off nodes
![](https://www.wildflower.org/image_archive/320x240/JAM6151/6151_IMG00564.JPG)
Small, smooth, elliptic leaves on pale gray bark marked with white patches
There are many different cultivars though with columnar and dwarf forms being available.
![](https://www.wildflower.org/image_archive/640x480/CBR/CBR_IMG16309.JPG)
retains leaves in the winter
