##### Propagation and control
Propagation: primarily propagates via rhizomes (runners)
Control: keeping a shallow barrier around culms that extends a few inches into the soil. cutting rhizomes with a spade will also separate from the main root system before a new culm can be established.

### Seasonal Periods

Blooming time: blooms intermittently throughout the year dependent upon pollination via the wind
Growing Time: Evergreen
Hardy level: depending on clustering and how well established, can survive up to -20C 

**NOTE: this plant will weaken when it goes to seed and will be more vulnerable as a result. Either chop down to preserve resources within the root system or provide support via foliar sprays (if possible)**

## Ecological functions
It can be a good way to preserve water and soil on a particularly vulnerable location. Due to its fast spread, it will quickly stabilize soil resources. Can also shade out weeds and other invasive species.
