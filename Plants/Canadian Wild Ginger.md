### Description 
Height: 0.1 - 0.5m (growing at a slow rate)

3. The root of the plant is round and fleshy (like ginger), with dividing stem supporting a heart-shaped, deep green above and light below, soft, wooly, and handsomely veined (oop) leaf, there being two to a plant. The flower is one to a plant, small and of a brownish purple color, growing only a few inches high and sometimes becoming covered by the dead leaves that carpet the woods.

## Ecological functions
Can shade out the ground and compete for surface roots. 
Provides a water barrier that can cool the ground and keep it humid in the hot summer months and heat during the cooler fall months (specific heat of water property).
