## Overview 
In developing the technology that is needed to create an effective resistance industry, mapping out the complexity and fabrication techniques that are required will be necessary. There is no established pathway towards the creation of an effective base of manufacturing, fuel production, necessities in maintenance and research, and valuable materials from natural resources. 

This drawing serves as a baseline assessment of useful technologies to that end, but is not an authoritative source on all pathways to effective, grassroots, industrial development (in accordance with sustainable principles of energy production, land use, and remedial technologies as a focal point of work output).

##### Categories for industrial development
- **Refining**: Transmutation of materials into more usable forms or the facilitation of such. Machines in this category use heat, electricity, and mechanical means to augment the structure, physical and/or chemical of input materials (either natural or from previous refining processes)
- **Diagnostics**: Measurements that confirm physical and chemical properties of materials
- **Maintenance**: Upkeep of other machines. Cleaning, repairs, and control of environmental factors responsible for degradation.
- **Cyclation**: The core function of the resistance industry. The act of cycling materials, energy, and processes into one another is potentiatied by technology within this category. Typically cyclation is of nutrients but can also involve material processing from refining processes and manufacturing.
- **Fuel and Energy Production**:  Another core function of the resistance industry. Without energy, motion cannot be harnessed for the sake of perpetuating OUR movement! Fuel is an essential resource that is both versatile and requires effort to harness in powerful forms.
- **Specialty Manufacturing**: Specific manufacturing processes that create components for finished ends, fabrication, or the other categories above.
