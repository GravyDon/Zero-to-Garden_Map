#editor you can switch between different configurations for your obsidian notes with three simple steps **WARNING! THIS WILL CAUSE YOUR CURRENT CONFIGURATION TO DISAPPEAR! SAVE THE WORKSPACE FIRST**:

### Saving your current workspace
- ctrl + p 
- Type "Manage Workspace" and click on the only option
- Type in the bar the name of the workspace that you want to save (this will save your current windows, and all the tabs that you have open)

### Opening a new workspace
- repeat steps 1-2
- now, click "Load" on one of the templates

BOOM! now you have a new workspace that has the files open for a specific kind of research. 
This can be highly effective to switch between tasks from session to session allowing for a customization of what specific topics you want to focus on from day-to-day or even do organizational work. #editor or you can switch back to the welcome workspace :3
