- Damping off -
- #Blight - Either two common fungal diseases or most commonly observed serious diseases on [[Tomato|Tomatoes]], [[Peppers (general)|Peppers]], [[Eggplant|Eggplants]], [[Potato|and potatoes]][^1]
	Usually begins at the base of the plant, and can easily result in total defoliation of lower leaves and the subsequent spread of fungal and or viral pathogens through the air and the xylem. [[Plant diseases and maladies#Altenaria solani|*Altenaria solani*]] is a mold that is associated with causing this disease in tomatoes
- #plantDiseaseCure/Blight : need to burn or destroy the plants in a way that does not spread disease
	

## Molds and fungi

- #plantDiseasePrevention/molds : **Do not water the leaves**, and make sure they dry after with a proper current of air, evaporation, etc. Best to water at the base of the plant rather than splashing water on the leaves. Plants probably do love the feeling though lol. **Too great a humidity between the ground and the underlayer of the leaf can cause this disease to arise.** -> *bio-available copper used in the environment can help disrupt metabolism of molds*
##### Altenaria solani
- most commonly associated with disease in tomatoes or potatoes

- #diseaseCure/Blight required for this specific mold once it develops past the early stages -> destruction of the affected plant
