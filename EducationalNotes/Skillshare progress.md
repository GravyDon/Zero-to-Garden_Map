# Overarching goals

For the entire skillshare series, of which I do not know how many I will be able to do, I am anticipating that the skills to be shared will be sufficient to empower participants with the knowledge and skills to: 
- Manage their own garden or farm dependent upon ambition and commitment to building dual power systems (either on an individual scale or communal scale).
- Acquire materials and/or create inventions that can: rapidly process nutrients for plant usage, create value-added byproducts (i.e. methane), heat, cool, and mix materials for chemical reactions, and support key processes on a farm/garden.
