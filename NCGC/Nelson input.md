### 🌱 Vision Committee

Purpose:  
The Vision Committee leads the strategic direction and long-term planning of the Nashville Community Garden Coalition. This committee ensures that all programs and initiatives align with the coalition’s mission to promote community wellness, environmental sustainability, and food justice through urban gardening.

Key Responsibilities:

- Develop and refine the coalition’s mission, vision, and core values.  
      
    
- Identify long-term goals and milestones for community garden expansion and impact.  
      
    
- Lead strategic planning processes and annual visioning retreats.  
      
    
- Facilitate inclusive input from coalition members, community partners, and garden leaders.  
      
    
- Evaluate the effectiveness of ongoing programs and recommend improvements.  
      
    
- Stay informed on emerging trends and opportunities in urban agriculture and food equity.  
      
    

Ideal Members:

- Big-picture thinkers  
      
    
- Strategic planners  
      
    
- Individuals with experience in nonprofit development, sustainability, or urban policy
    

  

### 🗂️ Admin Committee

Purpose:  
The Admin Committee manages the internal operations, documentation, and organizational infrastructure that support the effective functioning of the Nashville Community Garden Coalition. This committee ensures that records are maintained, communication flows smoothly between committees, and the coalition operates with accountability and transparency.

Key Responsibilities:

- Maintain up-to-date records, including meeting minutes, membership rosters, and policies.  
      
    
- Coordinate scheduling and agendas for coalition-wide meetings.  
      
    
- Ensure timely follow-up and task tracking across committees.  
      
    
- Support compliance with any legal, fiscal, or nonprofit reporting requirements.  
      
    
- Help onboard new members and maintain internal communication systems.  
      
    
- Collaborate with other committees to support cross-functional operations.  
      
    

Ideal Members:

- Organized and detail-oriented individuals  
      
    
- Those with administrative, clerical, or operations experience  
      
    
- People comfortable with tools like spreadsheets, shared drives, or project management platforms
    

  

### 💰 Mobilizing Resources Committee

Purpose:  
The Mobilizing Resources Committee is responsible for securing the financial, material, and human resources needed to support and expand the work of the Nashville Community Garden Coalition. This includes fundraising, partnership development, and volunteer coordination.

Key Responsibilities:

- Research and write grants, sponsorships, and fundraising proposals.  
      
    
- Develop and maintain partnerships with local businesses, foundations, and individual donors.  
      
    
- Coordinate in-kind donations such as tools, seeds, compost, and materials.  
      
    
- Organize and manage volunteer recruitment, orientation, and scheduling for coalition-wide initiatives and events.  
      
    
- Build a robust volunteer network that reflects the diversity of the communities served.  
      
    
- Maintain records of volunteers and track engagement to recognize contributions.  
      
    
- Support donation drives and community campaigns to raise awareness and resources.  
      
    

Ideal Members:

- Fundraisers and grant writers  
      
    
- Volunteer coordinators or community organizers  
      
    
- Relationship-builders with strong communication skills  
      
    
- People with nonprofit, development, or HR experience
    

  

### 📣 Events & Communication Committee

Purpose:  
The Events & Communication Committee oversees public engagement through events, outreach, and storytelling. The committee amplifies the coalition’s impact and builds community through dynamic programming and clear communication.

Key Responsibilities:

- Plan and coordinate community events, workshops, and volunteer days.  
      
    
- Promote events and garden projects via social media, newsletters, and local media.  
      
    
- Create engaging content that highlights garden stories, partners, and community impact.  
      
    
- Develop flyers, posters, and other outreach materials.  
      
    
- Maintain the coalition website and social media accounts.  
      
    
- Serve as a liaison with local media, schools, and neighborhood groups.  
      
    

Ideal Members:

- Event planners  
      
    
- Graphic designers  
      
    
- Social media storytellers  
      
    
- Community connectors and enthusiastic communicators
